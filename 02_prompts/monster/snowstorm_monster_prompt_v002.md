# Snowstorm Monster Prompt v002 - Round Clumsy Villain

## 用途

用于生成更适合第一集的暴风雪怪设定图。该版本基于 v001 调整，目标是减少“史诗 Boss / 人形肌肉怪”的倾向，强化“巨大、笨重、圆润、儿童动画反派”的方向。

## 核心修正

- 保留巨大压迫感，但不能像恐怖片 Boss。
- 保留发光蓝眼睛，作为主要识别点。
- 身体更圆、更厚重，像一团巨大的旋转雪云。
- 手臂必须是云雾状、雪团状、模糊粗壮形态，不要人类肌肉手臂。
- 冰晶数量适中，作为点缀，不要变成尖刺怪。
- 表情可以有一点凶，但要略笨、略滑稽，像会被雪球反杀的儿童动画反派。

## 正向 Prompt

```text
A giant family-friendly snowstorm monster character design for a cute animated comedy short film. The monster is made of swirling snow, dark storm clouds, icy crystals, and soft snow mist, with two glowing blue eyes, a wide cloud-like mouth, and a huge round bulky storm-cloud body. It has soft rounded snow-cloud arms, vague and chunky, not human arms. The silhouette is simple, readable, heavy, and slow-looking, like a clumsy animated villain that looks powerful but a little goofy. Add only a moderate amount of icy crystals as decoration, not too many sharp spikes. The monster stands behind Antarctic icebergs, towering over the icy landscape, dramatic but safe for children, intimidating but not horror, not disgusting. Stylized Pixar-like 3D animation, icy blue Antarctic lighting, soft snow particles, cinematic composition, high quality creature concept art, full body, front view.
```

## 推荐参数

| 参数 | 建议 |
| --- | --- |
| aspect ratio | 1:1 或 3:4 |
| output | creature concept art |
| style strength | 中 |
| variation | 中低 |

## 使用说明

- 这一版用于和 v001 候选图对比，重点看是否更圆、更笨重、更适合喜剧反派。
- 如果结果太像人形巨人，继续强化 `huge round bulky storm-cloud body` 和 `not human arms`。
- 如果结果太可爱，强化 `towering over the icy landscape` 和 `intimidating but not horror`。
- 如果结果太尖锐，强化 `moderate amount of icy crystals` 和负向词 `too many sharp spikes`。
