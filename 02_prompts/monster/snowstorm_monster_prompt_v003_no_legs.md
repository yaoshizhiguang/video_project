# Snowstorm Monster Prompt v003 - Powerful No Legs

## 用途

用于生成第三版暴风雪怪设定图。该版本综合前两版反馈：v001 的压迫感更好，v002 更儿童动画但压迫感不足且腿部形态出戏。v003 目标是保留 v001 的巨大压迫感，同时彻底避免人形腿、脚、裤子感下半身。

## 核心修正

- 保留 v001 的巨大体量、压迫感、冰山后升起的威胁感。
- 怪物下半身必须是旋转风暴云柱 / 雪雾漩涡，不要双腿、脚、裤子或人形站姿。
- 身体由乌云、旋转雪云、冰晶和雪雾组成，不能变成纯巨人或纯人形怪。
- 手臂可以粗壮，但必须是云雾状、雪团状、模糊形态，不要肌肉手臂。
- 保留两只发光蓝眼睛和云雾状大嘴，识别度要强。
- 仍然是儿童动画反派：强大、笨重、有压迫感，但不恐怖、不恶心。

## 正向 Prompt

```text
A giant family-friendly snowstorm monster character design for a cute cinematic animated short film, rising from behind Antarctic icebergs like a massive living blizzard. The monster is made of swirling snow, dark storm clouds, icy crystals, and soft snow mist, with two glowing blue eyes and a wide cloud-like mouth. It has a huge powerful storm-cloud torso, vague bulky snow-cloud arms, and a lower body that dissolves into a rotating tornado-like column of snow mist and storm clouds. No legs, no feet, no pants, no human lower body. The silhouette should be huge, intimidating, and readable, with strong scale and pressure, but still safe for children and slightly clumsy-looking like a bulky animated villain. Add icy crystals as dramatic accents around the shoulders and cloud body, not too many sharp spikes. Stylized Pixar-like 3D animation, icy blue Antarctic lighting, volumetric snow particles, cinematic rim light, high quality creature concept art, full body, front view.
```

## 使用说明

- 这一版优先解决“v002 腿部出戏”和“压迫感不足”的问题。
- 如果仍然出现腿，强化 `lower body dissolves into a rotating tornado-like column` 和负向词 `human legs, two legs, feet`。
- 如果太恐怖，强化 `family-friendly`、`safe for children`、`cute cinematic animated short film`。
- 如果太可爱没压迫感，强化 `massive living blizzard`、`strong scale and pressure`、`rising from behind Antarctic icebergs`。
