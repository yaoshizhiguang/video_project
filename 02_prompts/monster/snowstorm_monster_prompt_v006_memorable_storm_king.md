# Snowstorm Monster Prompt v006 - Memorable Storm King

## 用途

用于生成更适合短视频开场的暴风雪怪设定图。该版本吸收 v005 的剧情功能方向，同时补强“记忆点”和“第一眼吸睛”：它不是普通雪云怪，也不是史诗最终 Boss，而是“南极暴风雪之王，一个圆滚滚、发着极光蓝光、戴冰晶王冠的笨蛋反派”。

## 核心修正

- 目标是短视频 0-10 秒登场时抓住观众眼球：第一眼震撼，第二眼好笑，第三眼能记住。
- 加入明确设计锚点：冰晶王冠、极光蓝发光纹路、大云眉毛 / 云胡子、呆笨自信表情。
- 保持剧情功能：它仍然是会被雪球包成雪人的笨重靶子，不是不可战胜的史诗 Boss。
- 身体结构接近三层圆滚滚雪云雪人：大圆头、圆胖上身、宽圆雪雾底座。
- 冰晶要漂亮、像天然王冠和肩部装饰，不能变成满身尖刺或冰甲。
- 下半身是宽软雪云底座，不是细长龙卷风，不是双腿。

## 正向 Prompt

```text
A clean single creature concept art of the Antarctic Snowstorm King, a memorable family-friendly villain for a cute Pixar-like animated comedy short film. He is not an epic final boss, but a huge round clumsy snowstorm boss who looks impressive at first glance, then funny and a little dumb on second glance. The design must be eye-catching and easy to remember.

The monster has a giant rounded snow-cloud snowman silhouette, with a large round head, a bulky round upper snow-cloud body, and a wide soft swirling snow-cloud base that looks easy to wrap into a giant snowman later. No legs, no feet, no sharp tornado funnel. His body is made of dark storm clouds, fluffy white snow, soft snow mist, and a few beautiful blue ice crystals.

Give him strong visual anchors: a natural blue ice crystal crown on top of his head, a few elegant blue ice crystal shoulder ornaments, subtle aurora-blue glowing lines swirling around his snow-cloud body, oversized fluffy white cloud eyebrows, and a big curled cloud beard / cloud mustache. His eyes glow bright aurora blue, but the expression is goofy and overconfident, like “I am scary, right?” while actually being slow and dumb. Use a soft oval cloud-mouth with a silly grin or harmless open “aww” expression, no teeth, no sharp mouth edges.

The monster should feel large enough to scare tiny Antarctic animals, but still funny, rounded, and family-friendly. Keep the silhouette simple, readable, iconic, and toy-like. Antarctic icebergs frame the lower sides for scale without blocking the body. Clean centered creature design, cute cinematic 3D animation style, icy blue Antarctic color palette, aurora blue glow, soft snow particles, high quality character concept art, front view.
```

## 使用说明

- 使用时必须把“正向 Prompt”和“负向 Prompt”分别粘贴到生成工具对应的输入框。
- 这一版的重点是“记忆点”，不是单纯画质：冰晶王冠、极光蓝纹路、云眉毛、云胡子、呆笨自信表情必须出现。
- 如果结果太凶，强化 `goofy and overconfident`、`funny and a little dumb`、`family-friendly`。
- 如果结果太像冰巨人，强化 `giant rounded snow-cloud snowman silhouette` 和 `wide soft swirling snow-cloud base`。
- 如果结果太普通，强化 `memorable`、`eye-catching`、`natural blue ice crystal crown`、`aurora-blue glowing lines`。
- 如果工具没有单独负向 prompt 输入框，用 `Avoid: ...` 句式表达负向内容，不要直接粘贴负向词。
