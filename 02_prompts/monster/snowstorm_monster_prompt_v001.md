# Snowstorm Monster Prompt v001

## 用途

用于生成第一集反派“暴风雪怪”的第一版设定图。目标是做一个巨大、有压迫感、但仍然适合儿童动画的雪云怪物，不做恐怖片怪物或写实灾难云团。

## 设计目标

- 身体由旋转雪云、深色暴风云、冰晶和雪雾组成。
- 必须有两只发光蓝色眼睛，方便后续视频中保持识别度。
- 轮廓要简单清晰，有模糊粗壮手臂和云雾状大嘴。
- 气质是强大但笨重，像会被咕咕嘎嘎误打误撞打败的儿童动画反派。
- 整体要 family-friendly，不能过于恐怖、恶心或写实灾难片化。

## 正向 Prompt

```text
A giant family-friendly snowstorm monster character design for a cute animated short film, made of swirling snow, dark storm clouds, icy crystals, and soft snow mist, with two glowing blue eyes, vague bulky arm shapes, and a wide cloud-like mouth. The monster is huge, powerful, and intimidating, but still safe for children, not horror, not disgusting, slightly goofy and slow-looking, like a clumsy animated villain. Simple readable silhouette, standing behind Antarctic icebergs, icy blue Antarctic lighting, soft snow particles, stylized Pixar-like 3D animation, cinematic composition, high quality creature concept art, full body, front view.
```

## 负向 Prompt

```text
horror monster, scary horror, nightmare creature, bloody, gore, skull, corpse, zombie, sharp teeth, too many teeth, disgusting, realistic disaster, realistic tornado, realistic storm cloud only, human body, muscular body, complex armor, weapon, fire, lava, red eyes, multiple heads, extra limbs, overly detailed, unreadable silhouette, text, logo, watermark, blurry, low quality
```

## 推荐参数

| 参数 | 建议 |
| --- | --- |
| aspect ratio | 1:1 或 3:4 |
| output | creature concept art |
| style strength | 中 |
| variation | 中低 |

## 使用说明

- 第一轮先生成设定图，不要直接生成 9:16 分镜。
- 如果结果太恐怖，强化 `safe for children`、`cute animated villain`、`slightly goofy`。
- 如果结果太可爱，强化 `huge scale`、`towering behind Antarctic icebergs`、`powerful storm silhouette`。
- 如果结果太散，强化 `simple readable silhouette`、`two glowing blue eyes`、`vague bulky arm shapes`。
