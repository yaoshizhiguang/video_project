## Segment 01: 暴风雪怪出现

```text
Horizontal 16:9, 10-second cinematic 3D animation built from one fixed Antarctic master environment. Preserve the same camera angle, horizon line, foreground ice field, central blue iceberg group, far mountain silhouettes, animal staging, and wooden fish bucket position throughout the whole segment. The background must not change into a new scene.

0-2s: calm peaceful Antarctica under pale blue sky. Tiny penguins and seals rest on glossy ice, a wooden fish bucket sits quietly, large blue icebergs and distant snow mountains establish scale.

2-4s: the same scene begins to change. One side of the sky darkens, wind picks up, thin snow mist curls low across the ice, loose ice crystals drift upward, and the animals look up nervously. No full monster yet; only a subtle stormy snow-cloud core begins forming behind the mid-sized icebergs.

4-6s: the snowstorm monster begins forming as an elemental VFX event. Violent snow clouds, ground snow mist, cold wind streams, and floating blue ice crystals gather behind the mid-sized icebergs. Two bright blue glowing eyes appear first inside the snow cloud, then a dark cloud-hole mouth silhouette opens. The creature is still incomplete.

6-8s: the monster becomes mostly readable. Its upper body forms from thick dark storm cloud and rolling snow mist, ice crystals orbit and embed into the growing mass, and the lower body remains a rotating snowstorm base. The monster is huge compared with animals but clearly smaller than large icebergs and far snow mountains.

8-10s: the monster fully emerges and performs its first attack. From its wide dark cloud-hole mouth, a concentrated blue-white blizzard blast shoots sideways and shatters a small nearby iceberg into sparkling ice chunks. Tiny penguins and seals flee below, the wooden fish bucket slides across the ice. Clear cause and effect: peaceful scene -> crisis begins -> storm gathers -> monster forms -> first attack. Family-friendly Pixar-like 3D animation, icy blue palette, cinematic lighting, smooth continuous camera push-in, no text, no watermark.
```

镜头：one continuous wide establishing shot with a slow push-in, no hard cuts; slight camera shake only at the final blizzard impact.
